Who we are

What this website collects

The booking form and the shop checkout ask for your name, contact details, delivery address and the details of your request. When you press send, that message opens in WhatsApp or your email app and goes to us directly. This website does not store it on a server.

Your shop cart is saved only in your own browser so it is still there if you refresh the page. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser data.

How we use your information

We use it to reply to you, prepare quotes, arrange shipments, orders and services, and keep records of the work we do for you. We do not sell your information.

Other services

This site is hosted on a third-party hosting service, and your messages travel through WhatsApp or your email provider, each of which has its own privacy practices.

Your choices
